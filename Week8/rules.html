Platform Rules

Code of Conduct
  • All players must treat opponents, teammates, and platform staff with respect. Harassment or threatening behavior results in immediate suspension.
  • Cheating, exploiting bugs, or using third-party software for unfair advantage results in a permanent ban with no appeal.
  • Players must be at least 13 years old to register on CombatMatch.
  • Account sharing is not allowed. Each account must represent one individual player.
  • Quitting mid-match without an agreed forfeit counts as a loss and triggers a 24-hour matchmaking ban.
Team Registration
  • Teams need a minimum of 4 players and a maximum roster of 8 (4 starters + 4 substitutes).
  • Team names and tags must be unique and free of offensive language. Tags are 2-5 uppercase characters only.
  • Team captains are responsible for accepting match tickets and reporting results within the required window.
  • Roster changes are locked 24 hours before a scheduled tournament match.
Match Scheduling
  • Once both teams accept a match ticket, the schedule is final. Rescheduling requires mutual agreement at least 2 hours before match time.
  • Teams have a 15-minute grace period after the scheduled start. Failure to show results in a forfeit loss and a 150-point deduction.
  • All ladder matches must be completed within 7 days of the ticket being accepted.
  • Match results with screenshots must be submitted within 1 hour of the match ending.

Filing a Dispute

Valid Reasons to File a Dispute
  • Incorrect score was submitted by the other team.
  • Suspected cheating — a video clip or VOD is required as evidence.
  • Opponent failed to show up and did not submit a proper forfeit.
  • Ineligible player was used who was not on the registered roster at match time.
Dispute Process and Timeline
  • Step 1 — File within 2 hours: Disputes must be submitted within 2 hours of the result being posted. Late submissions are not accepted.
  • Step 2 — Upload evidence: Screenshots, clips, or scoreboard images are required. Disputes without evidence are automatically denied.
  • Step 3 — Staff review (24-48 hours): Both teams can submit responses during this window.
  • Step 4 — Final ruling: The decision is binding and cannot be appealed unless there is documented staff error.
Penalties for Abuse
  • Filing a dispute without legitimate grounds results in a 72-hour matchmaking ban for the team captain.
  • Repeated frivolous disputes lead to escalating penalties up to account suspension.
